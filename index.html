Questions

How do I place an order?

Add the pieces you want to your order, fill in your delivery details, and the order sheet gives you a message to send to us on WhatsApp. Your order is confirmed once we reply.

Can I buy and rent in the same order?

Buying and renting are two separate orders. Place one for the beads you want to keep and another for the ones you want to rent.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. Nationwide delivery can be offered, with cost and timing depending on destination. We confirm the delivery cost when we reply to your order.

How long does delivery take?

Same-day or next-day delivery can be available in selected locations. Other locations depend on the courier.

How does renting work?

See the rental page for pricing, deposits, returns and late fees.

Before you rent

How is the rental price worked out?

Each piece is priced per 3 days. A longer rental is charged in extra 3-day blocks. The order sheet shows the total before you send anything.

Is the security deposit refundable?

Yes. It is refunded after the jewellery is returned and inspected, provided it is in the agreed condition.

What if I return it late?

A late-return fee may apply according to your rental agreement.

What if the jewellery is damaged or lost?

Repair or replacement costs may be deducted from the security deposit according to the rental terms.

Can I rent more than one piece?

Yes. Add as many available pieces as you like to one rental order. They share the same delivery and return dates.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. Nationwide delivery can be offered, with cost and timing depending on destination.
